Engage Island — preview

Review page for Greg. The links open the real game in this tab; use Back to return here.

Start with the Friend picker Choose a Friend → the boat opening → the Island. Reduced motion Same start, no boat travel: straight to the Island after choosing. Replay the opening with Doug Skips the picker and plays the boat opening again.

While playing

  • Tap anywhere during the boat opening to skip to the dock.
  • On the Island, close Captain Pete's card with ✕, then tap the ground to walk.
  • The speaker button (bottom-right) mutes sound. Mute is remembered by this browser.
  • To replay from the picker, reload the page — the preview does not remember a Friend between loads.

Please know

  • Sound is placeholder only: a short tone for Captain Pete's greeting. There is no voice, music or boat sound yet.
  • This is a local preview, not a released build. How smooth it feels on a real iPad or phone has not been checked yet — that is what your try-out is for.
